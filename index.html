      📲 Save as an app

      Put Happy Hour on your home screen like any other app. It's free, there's nothing to download from an app store, and it takes about 10 seconds.

      1. Open this page in Safari.
      2. Tap the Share button . On newer iPhones, tap ••• at the bottom first, then Share.
      3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen ⊞.
      4. Tap Add. Look for the 🍻 Happy Hour icon on your home screen.

      Using Chrome on iPhone? Tap the Share button in the address bar, then Add to Home Screen.

      1. Open this page in Chrome.
      2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
      3. Tap Add to Home screen (it may say Install app).
      4. Tap Install (or Add). Look for the 🍻 Happy Hour icon on your home screen.

      Using Samsung Internet? Tap ☰ at the bottom, then Add page to > Home screen.
