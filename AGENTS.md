# AGENTS.md

Guidance for AI coding agents working on this repo.

- The owner is not a developer. Explain things in plain English with click-by-click steps.
- Keep it free: no paid services, plans or API keys.
- One page, no build step: `index.html` (plain JavaScript, no frameworks) and `venues.js` (the venue list the owner edits in GitHub's editor). Don't add npm, a bundler or a framework without asking.
- Only add venues with a real source link, known days and times, and a `checked` date. Never make up deals, times or venues; skip a venue if a detail is missing. Members-only club deals are included with `members: true`, which shows a "Members only" label.
- `lat`/`lon` are approximate (from the street address or suburb), so the app rounds distances ("about 5 min walk", "about 2 km away"). Directions search Google Maps by name and suburb.
- Tagline (welcome text, page description, manifest): "Every pub near you, plus the best happy hours."
- Look: fun and bold for 18–35s, a neon night-out theme (always dark, pink and cyan glows, Unbounded headings, emoji welcome). Colours are CSS variables in `:root`. `thumbnail.png` is the link preview (1200×630).
- Cities for now: Sydney and Melbourne (`CITIES` in `index.html`).
- `data/` holds the Greater Sydney and Greater Melbourne research as CSVs (venues and happy-hour sessions for each) with verification status; see `data/README.md`. Sessions marked `Listed (partial)` are not added to the app. In the Melbourne venues CSV, `Directory only` venues need a source before they can be added.
- Before pushing, open the page in Chromium (Playwright) at phone width (390px), in light and dark mode, and check there are no console errors.
- New suburbs need a region in `REGIONS` in `index.html` (for the Plan ahead area picker); unmapped suburbs fall under "Other areas".
- Saved spots live only on each phone (localStorage `happy-hour-saved`). `sw.js` caches the app network-first so it opens with poor signal.
- Nearby (formerly "Right now") and Map have a switch (`state.show`, saved): "🍺 All pubs & bars" (the default) or "🔥 Happy hours". The app's main job is finding a pub nearby; a happy hour is a bonus. All pubs & bars lists every bar, pub, nightclub and beer garden within the distance picked in the dropdown beside the search box (1, 2, 5, 10 or 20 km, default 2 km; `state.radius`, saved; a "Look within N km" button appears when fewer than `FEW` are found), of a searched place, you (Near me), or the middle of the chosen city, closest first, except that happy hours on now (then starting within the hour) within `BOOST_KM` (400 m, about a 5-minute walk) go first under a "🔥 Happy hour, a short walk away" heading: our venues (any status, labelled "🔥 Happy hour" with their badge and deal) mixed with OpenStreetMap's from the free Overpass API (no key; `OVERPASS` lists three servers, each given 20 seconds via `timedFetch` before the next is tried). It works anywhere in Australia, not only Sydney and Melbourne. OSM places matching our venues are left out; any OSM `happy_hours` tag is shown. Results are cached per place for the session. On now/Today/All week, the filter tiles and Pick for me only apply to Happy hours. The map shows OSM places as grey pins.
- The Map tab loads Leaflet 1.9.4 from cdnjs only when opened, with OpenStreetMap's own tiles (free, no key; darkened with a CSS filter on `.leaflet-tile-pane`, attribution kept). CARTO tiles now need a key, so don't use them. It uses the Right now filters. Happy hours with 30 minutes or less left show "Last call" (`LAST_CALL`).
- Search is the main way in ("Suburb, street or pub"). On Nearby and Map it always searches places (`placeSearch()`, whichever switch is on): typing only shows suggestions (suburbs, our bars, OpenStreetMap pubs already loaded nearby, and "🧭 … near …" for any place in Australia), and picking one or pressing Enter moves the list there (`state.focus`, cleared by the pink "📍 Place ✕" chip). Places are looked up with `findPlace`: OpenStreetMap's Nominatim, then Photon if that's busy (both free, no key, 8-second limit). In All pubs & bars, picking a bar shows the pubs around it; a group like Merivale switches to Happy hours; text that isn't a place filters pubs by name. Plan ahead still filters by name as you type.
- In All pubs & bars, OpenStreetMap pubs show "Open · till 1 am" / "Closed · opens 4 pm" from their `opening_hours` (`openStatus`; reads common day/time rules, days not listed are closed, anything else unknown). Filters there: 🟢 Open now (places known to be open, plus happy hours on now) and ❤️ Saved (any saved pub or happy hour, however far). Any OpenStreetMap pub can be saved with ♡ (localStorage `happy-hour-saved-pubs`).
- Distances read as walking time under 2.5 km ("6 min walk"; our venues rounded to 5 minutes because their positions are approximate), otherwise km.
- Map pins close together join into numbered bubbles (Leaflet.markercluster 1.5.3 from cdnjs, loaded after Leaflet; the map still works without it). Bubbles glow green if any pin inside is on now.
- Filters: 🌙 Late (runs past 9 pm, `LATE_FROM`) and 💸 $10 max (the deal mentions a price of $10 or less, `CHEAP_MAX`) sit in both panels. On now also lists "Starting within the hour" (`SOON_MINS`).
- Cards show "Wrong deal?" (a pre-filled GitHub issue) and warn "may have changed" when `checked` is over 180 days old (`OLD_AFTER_DAYS`).
- Shared links are `?bar=Name&city=City` and open straight to that bar.
- 🚶 Crawl (next to Pick for me, Nearby tab) plans a bar crawl. "Start from" takes a suburb, street (Nominatim lookup via `findPlace`) or bar, or 📍 Me (`state.crawlFrom`); with none it uses a searched bar or suburb, then your location, then wherever the most bars are on. Pick 2–5 bars and it builds a timed walking route (`planCrawl`): stops `STOP_MINS` (45) apart, a bar only if its happy hour is on when you'd arrive (and 20 minutes after), hops at most `HOP_KM` (1.5 km). The next bar is the lowest walk minus `DEAL_KM` per point of `dealScore` (prices listed, a price of $8 or less, kinds of drinks), so better deals are worth a few extra steps. Follows the drink/Late/$10/Saved filters. 🔀 Shuffle picks among the best few. The list shows a 📍 Start row, then each bar; each "🚶 min walk →" links to walking directions for that one leg. "Walk the route" is a Google Maps directions link from the starting point (coordinates for a suburb or street, the bar for a bar, left out for "where you are" so Google uses the phone's location) through every bar (`crawlLink`). Google Maps in a phone browser only takes 3 stops between start and finish; the app takes more, and the leg links always work.
- "📤 Share" (top banner and footer) shares `?get=app`: opening that link on a phone shows the "Save as an app" steps by itself. "📲 Get the app" opens the steps for iPhone and Android (a `<dialog>`). Websites can't add themselves to an iPhone home screen; on Android Chrome the tip's button becomes a one-tap "Install". The tip shows on phones until installed or closed (localStorage `happy-hour-install-hidden`). Both buttons hide once installed.
- Phones (touch screens) hide the page scroll bar; computers keep it. iPhones may still flash their own thin indicator while scrolling, like any app.
- `group` in `venues.js` names the hospitality group (only "Merivale" so far, for venues sourced from merivale.com). It shows on the card ("📍 Sydney CBD · Merivale") and search offers "All Merivale venues". Don't add a group without a source.
- Filters never scroll sideways. On now / Today / All week is a full-width three-way switch (`.seg-full`); drink and other filters are a 4-column grid of emoji tiles (`.tiles`), with a "✕ Clear" tile when any are on; Plan ahead days are a 7-column row (`.days`). The results heading shows a big count ("134 on now", `setCount`).
- Sorting (Nearby and Map): with Near me or a searched place, always closest first (each section: on now, starting within the hour, other bars). Without a location: on now first (ending soonest), then starting soonest.
- Daily specials: a venue can list `specials: [{ days, what }]` with a `specialsSource` link (only add with a source). Cards show "🎉 Today: …" for today's, then the rest. Search finds words in deals and specials: a word that's in our deals ("steak", "oysters") lists those happy hours (`dealWord`, `showDeals`) instead of being looked up as a place.
- Suggestions come in through GitHub issue forms (`.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/happy-hour.yml`, `daily-special.yml`), linked from the footer and the pubs list. Check each one before adding it.
- `.github/workflows/check-links.yml` runs `.github/scripts/check-links.mjs` on the 1st of each month (free for public repos; also runnable from the Actions tab). It checks every `source` and `specialsSource`, and lists pages that are gone (404/410 or the website no longer exists) in one open issue labelled "link check", closing it when all work again. If over a quarter fail at once it assumes a network problem and reports nothing.
- Searching a place (`focusOn`) always switches to All pubs & bars, so people see every pub there first; 🔥 Happy hours then shows only happy hours within the same dropdown distance of that place (or of you with Near me; `nowRows`). When there are none in range but some further away, `areaEmpty()` names the nearest and offers "Look within N km" and "🍺 Show all pubs & bars". The distance dropdown shows on Nearby and Map whenever there's a place.
